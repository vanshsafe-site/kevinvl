K.E.V.I.N — a private emotional-support AI chat that runs in your browser

K.E.V.I.N stands for Keeping Every Voice in Need. It is a free chat companion that listens patiently and responds with warmth and without judgment. It runs on your own device, so there is no account, no API key, and your messages are not sent to a server.

  • Private: the AI model runs locally in your browser.
  • Gentle: choose a listening, practical or short reply style.
  • Calming: built-in breathing exercises.

K.E.V.I.N is an AI, not a therapist or an emergency service. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
